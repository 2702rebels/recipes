This site collects the practices and patterns Team 2702 (Rebels) relies on: how we write robot code, how we tune mechanisms, and the training material we use to bring new members up to speed. Much of it is specific to our setup (CTRE Phoenix 6 with Pro-licensed Krakens), but most of it applies to any team.
